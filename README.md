One image summing up the world's news of **Sunday 22 November 2026**.

**The angle — closing results, not morning wires.** The two leads are the day's
final outcomes: COP31 collapses in Antalya with **NO DEAL** (longest summit ever,
first collapse since Copenhagen 2009), and House Democrats reach **258 seats**,
their largest majority since 1992 (Hubbard beats Foxx in NC-5 by 330 votes after
a recount). Around them: 30 stories across 6 regions, each labelled
REPORTED / CLAIM / DENIAL / ONGOING with its source, a numbered world map,
an hourly UTC timeline, a "NOT YET" strip for what had not happened, and a
date-check footer naming what was excluded (Shenzhen trilateral, 19 Nov).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`; both runs write identical bytes.
